Our story

Near the equator, the moon smiles.

In the north, the crescent moon stands upright. In Colombia, close to the equator, it lies on its back — horns up, like a cradle, like a smile. That smiling crescent is our logo.

Look closer and the rest of the moon is still there, glowing softly with earthshine. Nothing is hidden. That full circle is our promise: open code, honest software, and a whole that holds together.

The moon makes no light of its own; it reflects the sun's and shares it with everyone. Free software works the same way — and so do we.

Mission

Build reliable, secure software that respects people — and share as much of it as we can as free software that anyone can audit, improve and reuse.

Vision

By 2030, to be a Latin American reference for trustworthy, beautifully crafted software — proving from Colombia that technology can be open, secure and worthy of trust.

Meet Lino

Our mascot was hiding in plain sight.

Lino is an olinguito, a small night animal from the cloud forests of the Colombian Andes. Science only described it in 2013 — specimens had sat in museums for decades, mislabelled, until someone curious looked closely.

He comes out when the moon does, he is curious and patient, and his name carries a wink: o-LIN-guito, like Linux.
